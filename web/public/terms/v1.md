# SwarmTales Terms of Use

Version 1. Test version: SwarmTales currently runs on the Koinos testnet. These terms will be reviewed by a lawyer before SwarmTales launches on the main network, and that review may change them. When the terms change, the version number goes up and you will be asked to accept the new version before you write again.

## 1. What SwarmTales is

SwarmTales is a place to write stories together, one part at a time. It is for creative fun. It is not an investment, and nothing on SwarmTales is offered as one.

SwarmTales runs on the Koinos blockchain, a public record. Stories, credits, and everything described in these terms happen through public smart contracts. The website is a window onto them.

## 2. Who can use it

You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you live, to write, open stories, or buy anything on SwarmTales. Anyone may read.

## 3. Your wallet

You sign in with your own Koinos wallet. You are responsible for it and for everything signed with it. SwarmTales never asks for your private key or recovery words, and cannot recover them for you.

## 4. Your writing

You keep the copyright in what you write.

When you add a part to a story, you give SwarmTales and every co-writer of that story a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, permanent permission to:

- store, show, copy and share your part as part of the story;
- edit it lightly as the story's rules allow, while keeping your name on it;
- include it in the finished book, its PDF, and keepsake or collectible editions of that book;
- let other writers continue the story from it.

This permission cannot be taken back, because the story and its record are permanent and shared.

## 5. Only post what you have the right to post

Every time you add a part, you confirm that:

- you wrote it yourself, or you have the right to use it;
- any short quote from someone else's work is credited;
- it does not break anyone's copyright, privacy, or other rights, and it is not unlawful.

Do not paste text from books, articles, websites, songs or other works that belong to someone else. Works whose copyright has expired (in the United States, works published in 1930 or earlier) may be used, and should be credited.

If an AI tool wrote any of your part, tick "Written with AI help". You are still responsible for what you post.

## 6. Everything is public and permanent

Stories, parts, edits, reports and moderation actions are recorded on a public blockchain. Even private stories are readable by anyone who reads the blockchain directly; "private" only limits who can write.

If a part is removed, it disappears from SwarmTales, the contract's storage, and future editions of the book. Copies in the blockchain's transaction history cannot be erased by anyone.

## 7. Copied text and other problems

SwarmTales checks new writing automatically and listens to reports from readers and rights holders.

- A part may be hidden while it is checked. Hidden parts do not appear on SwarmTales or in finished books, and a story cannot be finished while a part is hidden.
- A part that copies someone else's work, or is unlawful, may be taken down for good. Taken-down parts are not refunded.
- Each takedown counts against the writer. After three takedowns, the wallet can no longer open stories, write, or report on SwarmTales. This is our repeat-infringer policy.
- Every hide, takedown and block is recorded publicly on the blockchain, with the reason.

How to report copied text, and how rights holders can send a copyright notice, is explained on the "Report copied text" page.

## 8. Ink, memberships and payments

Ink is a writing allowance for SwarmTales. It has no cash value. It cannot be sent, sold, traded, or exchanged for money.

Memberships, Ink packs, and story fees pay for using SwarmTales. They are not investments and earn nothing. Payments are final, except where the law says otherwise. On the testnet, all payments use play money.

## 9. Things you must not do

Don't use SwarmTales to harass people, post spam, share someone's private information, post sexual content involving minors, threaten violence, or break the law. Don't try to break, overload, or trick the contracts or the website.

## 10. No guarantees

SwarmTales is provided as it is. It runs on public software and a public network that may have errors, delays, or outages. As far as the law allows, SwarmTales is not responsible for losses caused by them, by your wallet, or by what other people write.

## 11. Changes

When these terms change, the version number goes up. The new version and its fingerprint are recorded on the blockchain, and you will be asked to accept it before you write again.
